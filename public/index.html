What is OLU-XMD?

OLU-XMD is a WhatsApp automation platform that provides a dashboard for connecting and managing WhatsApp bot features.

Can small and medium-sized businesses use OLU-XMD?

Yes. OLU-XMD is intended for entrepreneurs and businesses that want to explore WhatsApp automation as part of their customer communication workflow.

Is WhatsApp device linking free?

Yes. Linking your WhatsApp device does not require a subscription or wallet funding.

Do I need to create an account before connecting WhatsApp?

Yes. Enter your WhatsApp number here and select Generate Link Device. You will be taken to login or registration before connecting your device.

Will my WhatsApp number be saved?

Your entered number is temporarily saved in your browser for the onboarding process so it can be filled into the dashboard after you sign in.

Can I reconnect a WhatsApp account I linked before?

Yes. Once logged in, use the Reconnect Saved WhatsApp option on your dashboard when your saved session needs to reconnect.

Do I need a subscription to explore OLU-XMD?

You can link your WhatsApp without a subscription. Some premium bot features may require an active subscription.
